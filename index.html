How the model works
Handover = wargame handover + slip. Slip = delays before the assault (build-up − 7, lift hold, launcher days, ISR days (full in ECA B, half in ECA A), strike slip, sea-state days) + max(0, MCM days − MI slack). OCA 1 masses 4 Div a day before its assault (shuttle ~D+8 + 7 days = ~D+15 against the D+16 assault), so its first day of pre-assault delay is absorbed. OCA 2 launches FRACTURE the day 4 Div is massed, so it has no spare day.
Strike slip = strike-led days on the critical path × (1 ÷ effectiveness − 1). OCA 1: SHOCK to OE2 (7 days in ECA A, 10 in ECA B); ISOLATE runs to the fixed D+16 assault date, so small delays there are absorbed. OCA 2: OE2 (3 / 10 days) plus the 5-day EXPOSE & NEUTRALISE, which gates ROLL UP with no slack. When strikes beat plan, OCA 1 gains nothing in ECA A because its assault is tied to the D+16 terms deadline, and at most the ~3 days SHOCK ran late in ECA B. OCA 2 gains only on OE2, because EXPOSE & NEUTRALISE is a fixed window. When launchers survive longer, OCA 2's first echelon, already at sea in BrahMos reach, loses lift (model rate); OCA 1's dispersed lift only waits. Effectiveness = hit rate × min(sorties, tankers ÷ 8) × (1 − 2 × weekly aircraft loss × SAM factor) × (100 ÷ RAF generation) × (100 ÷ SAM effectiveness), with the last two capped at 1.25.
102 Corps full effect = deploy day + 19. The wargame already assumed deployment from 30 Oct (D+15); deploying 2 or more days earlier caps a Favourable result at Contested (DP5 at risk), and full effect by D+30 means DP7 is not met.
PGM reserve left = stock ÷ 80% − 75% ÷ hit rate, shown against the planned 25% reserve.
Lift left below the floor triggers S3 (OCA 1) or S2 (OCA 2): isolation-led, D+30 missed.
Rating: 2+ days in hand favourable; 0–1 contested; late unfavourable (wargame method).

How each number is worked out
Chance of success by D+30 = share of runs in which GL, WATSONS and MI are handed over by D+30, the lift left is at or above the landing floor, and PGMs last. Each run draws handover inside the wargame range. It then varies build-up (±2 days), strike effectiveness (±15%), sea state (0 days in 80% of runs, 1–2 days otherwise) and mine-lane overrun (0–2 days), plus the what-if slider settings. Lift holds and moved launchers in ECA B are already in the wargame result, so they are not added again.
Lift lost = a draw inside the model range for that OCA and ECA, plus the what-if extra. For OCA 2 only, add the model rate for each extra day the launchers survive, because its first echelon is already at sea in BrahMos reach.
Aircraft lost = base weekly attrition × days in contested air ÷ 7, scaled by RAF generation and SAM effectiveness. Air superiority comes ~D+7 in OCA 1 and ~D+17 in OCA 2 (deck).
Force ratio at the assault = force ratio ÷ (1 − 301 Div degraded before the assault).
4 Div casualties use Lanchester's square law with the defender 3× as effective in prepared positions. 301 Div breaks at 40% losses. Attacker losses = 1 − √(1 − 0.64 × 3 ÷ ratio²).
Loss-exchange ratio = RF losses ÷ BF losses in the assault on 301 Div (40% of 301 Div ÷ 4 Div losses).
PGM drawdown = 75% of stock planned by the decisive phase ÷ hit rate × (80% ÷ stock), + 2% of stock per extra strike day.

How to use this dashboard

1 · Pick a pairing

  1. Choose our course of action (OCA 1 or 2; OCAs 3–5 are unfavourable because they failed screening) and the enemy's (ECA A most probable, ECA B most dangerous; C and D are unfavourable to RF, so they were assessed, not wargamed). You can also click any cell in the All pairings grid.
  2. The four tiles give the D+30 result, the handover date, the days in hand and whether RF is still a threat at D+30 (DP7).

2 · Play the wargame

  1. Press ▶ Play (or space). The campaign clock runs day by day from D-14. The gold playhead moves along the timeline, and each phase opens as the clock reaches it.
  2. When the clock passes a decision point, the ★ lights up and the decision shows above the timeline.
  3. Use ◀ Phase / Phase ▶ (or ← →) to jump phase by phase while you talk. Speed sets the pace. Click a bar on the timeline to open that phase.
  4. Each phase shows our action → RF's reaction → our counter-action, the conditions and the outcome. Click a B (branch) or S (sequel) chip to see its trigger, decision point and action.

3 · Test an assumption (what-if)

  1. Move a slider on the right, such as build-up, tankers, SAM effectiveness, sea state or 102 Corps' deploy date. The tiles, handover date and triggered branches update, and the box below the sliders says why.
  2. Reset to wargame returns to the syndicate's wargame result.

4 · Compare the OCAs

  1. Open OCA 1 vs OCA 2 · the numbers. It runs both OCAs against both ECAs 2,000 times with your current slider settings. It gives the chance of success by D+30, expected handover, lift lost, aircraft lost, force ratio at the assault, 4 Div casualties, loss-exchange ratio and PGM drawdown.
  2. Set the weight on ECA B to show how much you plan against the most dangerous ECA.
  3. Loss and attrition figures come from labelled model settings, not the wargame. Say so if asked, and change the setting live to show how robust the result is.

5 · Check an assumption

  1. The Assumptions tab lists the deck's planning assumptions and the model settings. Search it, or tick Critical only.
