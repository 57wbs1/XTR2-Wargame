# XTR 2 · Syndicate 1 · Wargame Dashboard

Interactive view of Syndicate 1's Requirement 6 wargame (57 CSC, Exercise TIARA 2). Fictional exercise scenario.

- Pick an own course of action (OCA 1 to 5) and an enemy course of action (ECA A to D). OCA 1 and 2 against ECA A and B are the four wargamed pairings. ECA C and D are assessed (syndicate judgement, not wargamed); OCAs 3 to 5 were screened out at OCA development.
- Step through each phase: our action, RF's reaction, our counter-action, the conditions recorded and the outcome.
- Click a branch or sequel to read its register entry; click a star for the decision point, PIR and FFIR.
- The what-if panel moves planning assumptions (build-up, lift holds, MI mine lane, force ratio, weapon hit rate, strike sorties, tankers, ISR time to find moved launchers, RAF sortie generation, RF SAM effectiveness, launcher kills, PGM stock, lift and aircraft losses, landing floor, days lost to sea state, 102 Corps deployment day, RF reaction, RL accepting terms) and shows the effect on handover, the PGM reserve and the branches or sequels triggered. Strike, PGM, RF air threat and loss sliders are relative to plan because the deck gives no target counts or munition quantities. It is planning sensitivity, not a new wargame.

Keys: arrow keys step phases, space plays, 1-5 pick the OCA, A-D pick the ECA.

Single file, no build: open `index.html`.


## Tabs (29 Sep, aligned to the submission deck)
- **Wargame**: pick an OCA and an ECA, press Play for the campaign clock, and step through action, reaction and counter-action. Branch, sequel, DP and DPT numbers follow the OCA 1 and OCA 2 line-of-operation slides in the submission deck.
- **OCA 1 vs OCA 2 · the numbers**: 2,000 runs per pairing give the chance of success by D+30, handover, lift lost, aircraft lost, force ratio, 4 Div casualties, loss-exchange ratio and PGM drawdown. Loss and attrition inputs are labelled model settings (planning assumptions), not wargame results.
- **Assumptions**: the deck's planning-assumption register in plain words, plus the model settings.
- **How to use**: steps, keys and a 2-minute demo.
